About and criteria

How this directory is made

Short version: we read each business's own website, write down what it says about calm, comfort and sensory needs, and tell you plainly what we have not checked.

Researched, not yet checked

Credentials are not yet checked. We have not verified insurance, professional registration (for example RIBA, ARB, NICEIC or APDO), trade accreditations or client references. A listing is not an endorsement. Please make your own checks before you hire anyone.

What we read for

  • Architects: wording about wellbeing, daylight, acoustics, comfort, biophilic or sensory design.
  • Lighting: warm, layered or circadian and tunable-white lighting, and control systems such as Lutron or KNX.
  • Acoustic, flooring and joinery: measured noise reduction, acoustic floors, natural materials, fitted storage that lowers visual clutter.
  • Decorators: low or zero-VOC paints, odour control, breathable or natural finishes, colour advice that considers light.
  • Organisers: experience with ADHD, autism and sensory overload, and a calm, non-judgemental approach.
  • Buying agents: buyer-only agents who ask how you live, and notice noise, light and the feel of a street.

How we research

  • Each listing was researched from the business's own website on 29 October 2026.
  • Phone numbers and emails are only those printed on the business's own website. Where we could not find one, we say so.
  • Sensory comfort notes quote or paraphrase what the site says. Where a note is our own inference, it is labelled.
  • Some businesses are based just outside Surrey or SW London and say they serve the area. They are marked as based nearby.

Corrections, removals and Featured Partners

If we have got something wrong, or you would rather not be listed, email harj@eudaspace.co.uk and we will change or remove it. Free listings are free. Featured Partner placement is paid, always labelled, and does not change what we write.

Who runs it

The directory is run by Eudaspace, a Surrey-based neurodesign studio, which is pinned at the top as Lead Neuroarchitecture Design Studio. Because Eudaspace works in this field, we say so openly. Visit eudaspace.co.uk.
