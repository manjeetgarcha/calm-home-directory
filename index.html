Calm is a design decision

Light, sound, air and clutter all act on your nervous system, whether or not anyone designed them to. For busy professionals and neurodivergent households that can be the difference between a home that carries some of the weight and one that adds to it.

Finding trades who think this way is hard. It is easy to end up with twenty bright ceiling spotlights when nobody asks how the room should feel. We read each business's own website and note, in plain words, what it says about wellbeing, light, sound, air, calm or neurodiversity, and where it says nothing.

We would rather understate than oversell. Where a note is our own inference, it says so.
